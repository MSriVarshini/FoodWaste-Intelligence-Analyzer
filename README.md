Food Waste Intelligence Analyzer

A Python-based data analysis project that analyzes food waste patterns, identifies high-waste areas, and evaluates the financial impact of food waste using data analysis and visualization techniques.

Project Overview:
Food waste is a major operational and financial challenge in the food industry. This project analyzes food production, sales, waste, customer volume, temperature, food categories, and locations to identify meaningful patterns and insights.

The project performs data cleaning, feature engineering, exploratory data analysis, statistical analysis, and interactive visualizations.

Objectives :
Analyze overall food waste.
Calculate the overall food waste percentage.
Identify food categories with the highest waste.
Identify locations with high food waste.
Find the most frequently wasted food items.
Calculate financial losses caused by food waste.
Analyze the relationship between temperature and food waste.
Analyze the relationship between customer volume and food waste.
Categorize records based on food waste risk.
